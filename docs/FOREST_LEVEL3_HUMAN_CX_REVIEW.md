## Child-CX acceptance questions

Mark each item **APPROVE** or **REJECT** and note the concrete reason:

- The full arc feels like one coherent 5–10 minute habitat-restoration adventure.
- L3 is materially different from L2 in problem, actions and consequence rather than being reworded questions.
- A young child can infer the next touch/action from the layout and object states.
- Touch targets are comfortable on a 360 × 640 phone.
- Honest retry feels supportive and retains successful work.
- Shelter repair, sorting, feeding and growing feel like at least three distinct interaction families/actions.
- The world visibly becomes more alive because of the child’s actions.
- Dheu, Scientu and Shaitanu each have a meaningful role in the arc.
- Reduced-motion mode preserves state meaning.
- Sound-off mode preserves every required instruction, retry cue and consequence.
- Completion/relaunch makes the changed Forest feel persistent rather than decorative.
- Discovery events feel like meaningful finds generated by the Forest, not a separate reward currency.
- Replay does not create extra discoveries or rewards.
